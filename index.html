About GDGOC

GDG on Campus (GDGOC) is a Google Developer Groups community for university students. It gives students a place to learn modern technologies, work with peers and grow as developers.

Through community activities, students can build projects, explore developer tools and meet others who share their interest in technology.

  • Learn modern technologies
  • Collaborate with peers
  • Build projects
  • Explore developer tools
  • Improve technical skills
  • Connect with developers
  • Grow professionally
